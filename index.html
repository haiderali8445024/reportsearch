How it works

Three steps, from a name or address to a report you can actually download.

  1. 1

    Enter a name or address

    Start with a full name and state, a street address, or a date of birth. The more precise the input, the fewer false matches.

  2. 2

    We point you to the right database

    Our guides map each record type to the office that actually holds it — county, state, federal or DMV — with fees and turnaround.

  3. 3

    Get your report

    Download instantly where records are digital, or follow the mail-in template we provide for certified copies.
